Lorenzo Faggi is a writer and filmmaker. He lives in Milan.

His artistic practice experiments with different forms and languages including documentary, fiction, podcast and collaborative forms of storytelling.

His films have been screened at national and international film festivals such as the Venice Biennale, Festival dei Popoli, Trento Film Festival, Full Frame Film Festival.

His podcasts have been featured on Il Post, Gruppo Gedi, Radio Rai and Il Sole 24 Ore.

He is the co-founder of Tracce.tv, a creative production agency specialising in visual communication. He works as a freelance writer for TV, media companies, private firms and cultural institutions.

With an emphasis on social communities and the 'liminal' conditions of living beings, his work seeks to find the universal in small stories by maintaining a peripheral vision and a lateral approach.
